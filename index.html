    Chapter 05 — The person

    Engineer by degree. Builder by habit.

    Every project here started in a domain I had to learn first — lending rules, clinic workflows, how parents choose a tutor. I don't wait to feel ready. I build, find out what I don't know, and close the gap.

    1. Build to learn. The first project in a new domain is the tutorial.
    2. Study what works. Take apart the best existing product, then do it clearer.
    3. Ship, then sharpen. A live v1 teaches more than a perfect draft.
