About

Engineer by degree. Designer by choice.

I studied electronics at NIT Kurukshetra, then realised I cared more about the person using a product than the circuit inside it. So I taught myself UX, front-end and data, and I learn each new domain by building in it: lending rules for Maitry Finance, clinic workflows for ClinicAI, how parents choose a tutor for Rinkal Coaching.

  1. Build to learn. The first project in a new domain is the tutorial.
  2. Study what works. Take apart the best existing product, then do it clearer.
  3. Ship, then sharpen. A live v1 teaches more than a perfect draft.
