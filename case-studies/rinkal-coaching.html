Putting a great home tutor online

Rinkal Ma'am teaches Maths, Science and English to Classes 8–10, but she had no online presence at all. Parents and students could only find her by word of mouth.

Role
Solo designer and developer
Client
Rinkal Coaching Classes
Built with
Vercel, Supabase
Scope
Website, enquiry flow, quiz, 30-second lessons

The problem

A home tutor lives on referrals. Without a website, a parent who heard her name had nothing to check, and a student had no way to see how she teaches.

The site had two audiences with different jobs. Parents decide and pay, so they need trust and clear answers. Students attend, so they need a reason to be curious.

My goals were to let her showcase her teaching, make enquiring about a batch effortless, and give students something fun to learn from.

Before and after

Before

  • No online presence
  • Found only through word of mouth
  • No way to preview her teaching
  • Enquiries only by phone, if at all

After

  • A site parents can check and share
  • Batch enquiries in about 20 seconds
  • Quiz and 30-second lessons for students
  • Enquiries tracked in Supabase

Design decisions

Send the enquiry through WhatsApp, pre-written

Parents pick class, board, subjects and preferred time, then tap “Send on WhatsApp”. The app opens with the message already written, and Ma'am replies personally with timings and fees.

Parents already live on WhatsApp. Don't make them learn a new channel.

Design for parents and students separately

Parents get “Why parents trust us”, a personal note from Ma'am and an FAQ that answers timings, fees and progress tracking. Students get a 60-second quiz and animated lessons.

Two audiences, two different jobs.

Show the teaching, don't describe it

Twelve 30-second lessons, like squaring numbers ending in 5 or why the sky is blue, act as a free sample of how she explains things, each ending with a quick check.

A sample lesson beats any claim about being a good teacher.

Build for word of mouth

A “Share with a friend” section is aimed at class WhatsApp groups, since that's how tutors actually get found.

Help the existing channel work better.

Create honest urgency

A countdown to the Class 10 board exams nudges parents to start early, with a clear note that the date is illustrative and should be checked against the official datesheet.

Urgency, without misleading anyone.

Data and what's next

Enquiries are logged in Supabase so the teacher can track who got in touch. The data is kept confidential. She now has a place to showcase her teaching, and parents and students have a way to reach her.

Next, I'd track which lessons get opened and shared most, and whether quiz-takers go on to enquire, then use that to decide what content to add.
